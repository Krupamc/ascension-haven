Ascended (Pre-Alpha-Beta ver)

Genre: Sidescroller 
Start your life as a frog in the overworld. Dodge the reeds and you'll ascend to heaven; run into trouble and you'll fall down beneath the Earth. Do not fret if you fail, there's always a way to bounce back.

Controls:
Keyboard: Arrows to move
